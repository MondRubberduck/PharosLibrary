1Where is your library?

Pick the folder that holds your assets: packs, projects, textures, sounds, animations, all of it. Pharos never writes into it except its own _Agent_Files folder (and Exports when you ask for a conversion). The registry, a small database Pharos builds for itself, goes somewhere else.

derived data, rebuildable; leave the default unless you know why

2What is where

One line per folder that is a thing: a pack, a project, a kit, a texture library. The survey reads the structure and tells you what it knows and what it guesses; correct the kind where it is wrong, untick Index for folders Pharos should leave alone, and answer the decisions (nothing with an engine runs without your word). A folder inherits the decision of the folder above it. Tick Confirmed when a line is right; Accept every guess does that for the rest.

3Index it

Runs everything that needs no engine: measures the models, lists the clips, reads the texture and sound folders, writes the agent's files into _Agent_Files. Minutes for a large library. The engine chains you approved are listed afterwards with their commands.
